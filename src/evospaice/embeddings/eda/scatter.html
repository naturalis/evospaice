Sampling, matching and scientific limitations

Reference tips and original embeddings are joined by exact record ID. A sampled record is a species-labelled BIN representative, not necessarily a unique species. Repeated species labels remain separate records; pairs between those records are identified separately.

Reference distances are patristic distances: the sum of branch lengths on the unique path connecting two original reference tips. Embedding distances are cosine distances between their original, L2-normalized vectors. Reference branch-length units are not specified by the source file.

Only pairs with known reference path lengths are plotted. A missing edge excludes paths crossing it, not every path below it. No unknown distance is replaced by zero in this analysis.

Pairs are not independent because they share sampled records. No naive pair-level p-values or confidence intervals are presented. Overall correlation may be dominated by between-family comparisons; inspect the within-genus and within-family filters as well. Correlation alone does not establish phylogenetic accuracy or a causal relationship.

All plotted points use float64 distances. Encoded data is embedded in this page; it works offline with no network requests or data uploads. Point opacity reveals overlap, but this is a scatterplot, not a statistical density estimate.

Uniform record sampling, exhaustive pairs within that sample. Pearson and Spearman correlations are invariant to positive changes of units; cross-unit raw RMSE is intentionally not reported.
