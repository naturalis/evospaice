A comparison, not an ancestry claim.

  • These are precomputed full-cohort trees. Controls reveal existing results; they do not refit representatives or run live NJ.
  • Each parent resolves its child representatives using neighbor joining, then grafts each complete child subtree. Taxonomy constrains grouping; embeddings resolve within that constraint.
  • Negative NJ edges are clamped to zero before midpoint rooting. Midpoint is a display/build convention, not biological ancestry. All-zero connectors retain their NJ root; unary edges are zero.
  • Cosine dissimilarity and Gaussian W2 have different units. Branch-length views use independent scales. Grafted paths may overcount original embedding distances.
  • Split differences are exact unrooted Robinson–Foulds (RF) on the visible, collapsed tips. Trivial splits are excluded; complementary root splits are counted once. Zero-length resolved splits are retained. This is not accuracy, calibration, or comparison against a biological reference.
