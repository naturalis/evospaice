# Phylogentic trees vs Taxonomic trees

See [Phylogentic tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylogenetic_tree) on Wikipedia and [Taxonomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomy) for more indepth explanations.

From this taxonomic tree we can learn that all these animals are cats
![](https://bigcatswildcats.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/big-cats-bio-classifcation-chart-768x543.png)


What we can read that since they are in the same genus, the Lion (Panthera leo) and Tiger  (Panthera trigris) are close realted. We can also see that the Cloud leopard (Neofelis nebulosa) must be a bit related to them since it is in the same subfamily. But remember, someone put them in that subfamily and when Linneaus named animals we had no DNA, often animals were placed based on looks, appearance, whatever. Nowadays based on DNA we can re-organize the taxonomic tree. 
_note: based on the nuclear DNA we know, although the clouded leopard is within the Pantherinea but not in the Panthera Genus. The closely related Sunda clouded leopard (Neofelis diardi) has the same nuclear DNA, but different mitochondrial DNA, so on a moleculair level they are quite similair. In taxonomy we can understand that since they are both in the same genus Neofilus. But what we cannot see what the difference is between a lion and a clouded leoard, and how far they diverged from their shared ancestor. We know that there was a species that eventually evolved into the Lion, the Cheeta and the Clouded Leopard, since they are all in the same family of Felidae. And to make things more complicated, there are clouded leopards on Borneo and on Sumatra, and based on DNA we can now say they are subspecies: Bornean clouded leopard (Neofelis diardi borneensis)
and the Sumatran clouded leopard (Neofelis diardi diardi)

We can also 'unnderstand' from the taxonomy that the Cheetah, Puma and our house cat are more closely related than a Cheetah and a Leopard (often mixed up by people based on the looks). This relationship can be defined based on DNA, or on that specific flexible bone in their neck (hyoid) which allows the Panthera tot roar, while Felinea meow.
