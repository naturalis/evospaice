## Hypothesis

We believe that by building a DNA reference tree and representing samples within it so that it captures meaningful evolutionary relationships, we can help scientists, researchers, and policymakers better measure and understand biodiversity data and make more informed decisions.

At a high level, we will validate this effort by measuring:

* Genetic similarity representation
* Algorithmic performance at scale
* Value for end users

This effort aligns with our broader vision of protecting life on Earth through a deeper understanding and appreciation of biodiversity.
